Conquer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy covers the Conquer app for iPhone and this website. Conquer is made by an independent developer, shown as the seller on Conquer's App Store page ("we", "us"). You can reach us at the email address at the bottom of this page.

The short version

  • Your screen time stays on your phone. We never see which apps you use or for how long.
  • The only thing that leaves your phone is your daily points total, and only if you play against a friend.
  • There is no sign-up: no name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete everything at any time with "Delete my data" in Settings.

Screen time (stays on your phone)

With your permission (Face ID or passcode), Conquer uses Apple's Screen Time tools. You choose apps and categories with Apple's own picker. Apple designed these tools so that apps never see the names of the apps you pick or the websites you visit. Conquer only learns when a group you chose has been used for another 15 minutes, and turns that into points on your phone.

Your minutes, tiers, and app choices never leave your phone. The same goes for Dynasty's tools (The Sentry, Fortress mode, Ceasefire, Banish, Treasury, Royal Advisor reports, Herald and Missions): they run on your phone only. You can turn off Screen Time access at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Playing against a friend

To run a battle, Conquer uses Google Firebase as its server. When you play a friend, the server holds:

  • an anonymous account ID created by the app (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID);
  • your final points total for each day, sent just after midnight;
  • which battle you are in and the day it started. Who won is worked out on each phone, not stored on the server.

Your friend sees only your daily points total. Never your minutes, apps, or tiers. Names are never sent: you name your friend yourself, on your own phone. Invite links are single-use, join one battle, and contain no name or points. Either player can end a battle at any time.

Daily totals are kept on the server for 40 days, so weekly and monthly results can be worked out, and are then deleted, unless you have Dynasty (below).

Dynasty subscription

Dynasty is an optional subscription bought through Apple. Apple handles all payments; we never see your card or payment details. Your Dynasty history is kept on your phone; the server still keeps daily totals for 40 days only.

Notifications

If you allow them, Conquer shows notifications such as the morning results. They are created on your phone, never include your minutes or apps, and can be turned off in Settings.

Usage counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counting service, on this website to see how many people visit and which features get used. The Conquer app does not use it. It does not use cookies, build a profile of you, or store your IP address, and it never receives your screen time or points. We only see totals. See goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

Who else handles data

Google (Firebase) stores the battle data above for us. Apple handles purchases. We share data with no one else, and never sell it or use it for advertising.

Delete my data

"Delete my data" in Settings erases your data on your phone and removes your account and daily totals from the server, and ends your battles. Deleting your data does not cancel Dynasty: billing continues through Apple until you cancel in your iPhone's Settings under Subscriptions.

Children

Conquer is for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Conquer, contact us and we will delete their data.

Your rights

You can see your data in the app and delete it at any time. For any other privacy question or request, email us. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights over your data, and we will honor them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and post the new version at this same link.
